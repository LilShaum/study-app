# Learn: what it lacks

For the owner. Short answers at the bottom.

## Fixed already (live)

Learn no longer asks about things a step hasn't shown. A card that names
none of the step's terms used to be dropped into a step by its position.
Now it goes with the term whose passage it comes from, and anything that
fits no term waits for a last step at the end of the section.

## What I found walking Learn on a phone

I went through one section (Michaelis–Menten, 2 steps) as a student would,
on the test course, getting some wrong.

1. **It asks for facts it never showed.** The reading cards are one-line
   definitions. The flashcards and questions then ask for things that are
   only in your notes: "What range do Km values fall in?" was never on any
   card. The passage it comes from is folded away under "From your notes".
   This is probably most of "not very in-depth".
2. **A step is long.** "4 terms" was 22 cards before misses, 32 with them.
   It doesn't feel like 4 terms.
3. **Misses can repeat back to back.** At the end of a step, a missed
   question came back three times in a row with nothing between, so you
   just click the answer you saw a second ago.
4. **A miss leaks into the next step.** A step 1 question came back after
   "Step 1 done", in the middle of step 2, still labelled "Step 1 of 2".
5. **No "next section" at the end.** Learn finishes with "Study again"
   (the same section). Today offers "Keep going". Learn should offer the
   next section.
6. **A miss shows the answer, not the lesson.** When you get a term wrong
   you see the right word, not the definition or the passage again.

## Learn compared with Today

| | Today | Learn |
|---|---|---|
| Fits your time | yes | no, a whole section however long |
| After a miss | comes back 3 cards later | same, but see 3 and 4 |
| Pause between steps | yes | yes |
| Finish screen | when to come back, Keep going, next course | when to come back, but no next section |
| Catch-up a few hours later | yes | yes (through Today) |
| Steered by the exam | yes | yes, picks exam sections first |
| Picks up where you stopped | skips finished steps | only if you left mid-session; otherwise starts the section again |

## What I'd build, in order

A. **Teach before asking (fixes 1).** In Learn, show a flashcard's answer
   the first time, as a reading card ("Read this, you'll be asked in a
   moment"), then ask it a few cards later. Also open "From your notes" on
   reading cards in Learn instead of folding it. Needs the simulator first.
B. **Smaller steps (fixes 2).** Cap a step at about 12 cards, splitting
   the questions over more steps.
C. **Misses stay in their step, with a gap (fixes 3, 4).** A miss comes
   back inside its own step, never twice in a row; if the step is ending,
   it comes back as the step's last card.
D. **Next section at the end (fixes 5).** "Next: section 3" on the finish
   screen, like Today's "Keep going".
E. **Show the definition after a miss (fixes 6).**
F. **Pick up where you stopped.** Learn skips steps you've finished, like
   Today.

C, D and E are small. A and B change what is asked and when, so I'd run
them through the simulator first.

## Your call

- Build all of A–F? Or only the small ones (C, D, E) first?
- For A: is showing the answer first, then asking, what you'd want?
