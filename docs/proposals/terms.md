# The app's own words

The owner, 2026-11-24: "when I read "14 fading" it's unclear what is fading
and what fading means. If we are gonna use new terms unique to the app, they
need to be explained in the on-boarding or something … "cards" seems maybe
specifically referring to flash cards so maybe items is better but like what
items, the graded ones only?"

## How each was decided

A word the app made up costs a new student a guess. Each term is kept only
if it says something plain words cannot, and then it is taught where it is
first met, by showing rather than a definition. Otherwise it is replaced.

| Term | Means | Decision | Where it is taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| fading | a part not practised for a while, slipping from memory; its leaves fall | **Kept**: it is the tree's own word, and the reason to come back | The first run: you leave a branch unstudied and watch its leaves fall. Then the lecture page's tree, whose leaves fall on opening |
| due | the questions the memory model says to review now | **Replaced** by "to review" ("5 to review", "Nothing to review", "Everything to review is done"). Two words for one idea was one too many | Needs no teaching |
| catch-up | the short second sitting for what came back since | **Replaced** by "a second look" in the lecture page's line | Needs no teaching |
| cards | the things in a lecture | **Replaced** by "questions" where they are answered (to review), "items" where it means everything in a section (reading as well) | Needs no teaching |
| Today | the day's sitting, review first then new | Kept: plain | The library's Today list says what each line will do |
| Learn, Read, Recall, Apply, step | Learn's order inside a section | Kept: the step line names all three at the top of each step, and the one you are in is struck | Where met, on the step line |
| sitting | one go at studying | Kept: a plain English word ("This sitting") | Needs no teaching |
| set aside | a card taken out because it is not on the exam or is wrong | Kept: the control asks "Not needed?" and then says "Set aside as …" | Where met |
| Not sure | answered right but unsure, so it comes back sooner | Kept: what it does is said beside it | Where met |
| held | still remembered (the tree's measure) | Kept only for screen readers (the tree's numbers' labels) | Not shown |

Practice's ready-made "Due now" and Help's sentences that say "due" are
changed with the Practice and Help work of the design update, so two
sessions do not edit the same file at once.
