BIOL 210 - Lecture 3: Membrane Transport

Learning objectives
  Explain how osmosis differs from simple diffusion.
  State what tonicity describes.

Simple diffusion is the net movement of a solute down its concentration
gradient, requiring no energy and no transport protein.

Osmosis is the net movement of water across a semipermeable membrane from a
region of low solute concentration to one of high solute concentration.

Tonicity describes the relative solute concentration of two solutions
separated by a membrane.

  Mode                Requires ATP        Moves against gradient
  Simple diffusion    No                  No
  Active transport    Yes                 Yes
